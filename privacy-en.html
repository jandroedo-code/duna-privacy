Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Publisher and contact

Duna: Calendario y Agenda is an application by Alejandro Edo. For privacy and support questions: infostudiovertigo@gmail.com.

Data used by Duna

Duna uses the events and agenda information you create within the application, along with preferences needed for it to work. Events are created within Duna; the app does not access your Apple calendars or reminders.

Purpose

This information lets you organize and view your calendar and agenda and maintain your data through iCloud. It is not used for advertising or sold.

Storage and iCloud

Data is stored on your device and in iCloud, associated with your Apple Account. Apple provides iCloud under its terms and privacy policy. Synchronization depends on your account settings, service availability and connection. Disabling synchronization does not mean data already stored in iCloud is deleted.

Collection and tracking

Duna does not use its own accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and does not send your agenda to developer-operated servers. Apple may process information relating to its services, backups and diagnostics under your settings and its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device and in iCloud until you delete it using the available options in Duna or Apple storage controls. Deleting the app from your device does not guarantee deletion of iCloud data or backups. You can manage iCloud storage and backups in your Apple Account settings.

Questions and rights

Contact the address above with privacy questions and, where applicable, to exercise rights of access, correction, erasure, objection, restriction and portability. You may also complain to your data protection authority. The developer does not hold a copy of your agenda on its own servers.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if Duna’s data practices change. The date at the top identifies the current version.
